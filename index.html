🛠️ How It Works

Imagine you have different AI models and you want to know which one is best at understanding Indonesian. local-llm-eval does three important things:

  1. Runs Tests: It asks each AI model the same questions and records the answers
  2. Checks Accuracy: It compares the AI's answers to correct answers and gives a score
  3. Shows Results: You see clear charts and tables showing which model is best

The app uses special mathematical methods (Wilson intervals and McNemar tests) to make sure the results are trustworthy. But don't worry - you don't need to understand these methods. The app explains everything in simple language.
